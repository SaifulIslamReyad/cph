Not :
    The ~ (bitwise NOT) operator in bit manipulation is a unary operator 
    that inverts all the bits of a number. This means:
        0 becomes 1
        1 becomes 0
    ~x = − ( 𝑥 + 1 ) 
    This formula works because of two’s complement representation in computers.
    5 in 8-bit binary:
    00000101
    Applying ~ (flip all bits):
    11111010
    This is the two’s complement representation of -6.

    Thus,
    -5 in 8-bit binary (two’s complement):
    11111011
    Applying ~ (flip all bits):
    00000100
    This is the binary representation of 4.
    Thus,
    ~(-5) = 4
